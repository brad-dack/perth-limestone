# Perth Limestone Group — Copy Revisions (Level 2.1)

Applies the seven fixes agreed after the Level 2 review. Everything here is a
drop-in replacement for the matching part of the Level 2 package. Nothing else
in Level 2 changes.

Summary of what changed:

1. Callback promise softened to a launch-safe version (site wide).
2. Privacy policy drafted as Page 7 (operator to review and place).
3. At-form collection notice drafted (operator to place beside every form).
4. Previous-role specifics downplayed (no named employer, title or years).
5. About page voice made consistent (first person plural throughout).
6. Mortgage-broker analogy replaced with a plain, non-fiduciary framing.
7. FAQ schema clarified — kept, but not relied on for rich results.

## 1. Launch-safe callback copy (site wide)

The current copy promises an outcome the business cannot yet deliver (no
contractor is signed). These replacements describe what we actually do, so the
site is truthful from day one and needs no rewrite when a contractor signs.

**Form microcopy, directly under the button — every page.**

- Old: *A local limestone contractor will call you back.*
- New: **We'll pass your job to a local limestone contractor in your area.**

(The second line stays exactly as written: *"We read every enquiry and come
back to you either way. If we can't match your job we'll tell you, and point
you somewhere useful."* — it is already truthful.)

**Homepage hero line.**

- Old: *Tell us about your job below and a limestone contractor will call you back to arrange a quote. There is no cost and no obligation.*
- New: **Tell us about your job below and we'll pass it to a limestone contractor working in your area to arrange a quote. There is no cost and no obligation.**

**Homepage "What happens when you enquire" list — steps 3 and 4.**

- Old:
  3. A limestone contractor working in your part of Perth calls you back to arrange a time to look at the job
  4. They quote it. You decide
- New:
  3. **We pass it to a limestone contractor working in your part of Perth**
  4. **They arrange a time to look at the job, quote it, and you decide**

### Body copy

# H1: Privacy policy

**Last reviewed: [DATE]**

This policy explains what we collect when you use this website, why we collect
it, and who we share it with. Perth Limestone Group is operated by
[OPERATOR NAME], ABN [ABN], registered business name [BN NUMBER].

## H2: What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form, we collect the details you enter: your name,
phone number, email address, suburb, and the description of your job (type, rough
size, timeframe). If you phone or email us, we have whatever you choose to tell
us. We do not collect anything else, and there are no accounts or logins on this
site.

The site may use basic analytics to understand how many people visit and which
pages they read. That data is aggregated and is not used to identify you.

## H2: Why we collect it, and who we share it with

We collect your enquiry for one purpose: to pass it to a limestone contractor
working in your part of Perth so they can contact you about your job and arrange
a quote.

**Your details are shared with that contractor.** That is the purpose of the
form, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Perth Limestone Group has a
commercial arrangement with the contractor, under which the contractor pays us
for the enquiry. You pay nothing. This is described in plain terms on the
[About page](/about).

What we do **not** do:

- We do not sell or broadcast your details to a list of businesses. One
  contractor receives your enquiry.
- We do not use your details to market unrelated products or services.
- We do not pass your details to anyone other than the contractor handling your
  enquiry, except where we are required to by law.

## H2: How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and for a
reasonable period afterwards for our own records. If you would like your details
removed, contact us using the details below and we will remove them.

## H2: Accessing or correcting your details

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us

address for privacy requests].

## H2: Complaints

If you are concerned about how your details have been handled, contact us first
and we will try to resolve it. You can also contact the Office of the Australian
Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

## H2: Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The review date at the top shows when it was last
changed.

## 3. At-form collection notice (place beside every form)

A short notice at the point of collection, so the person is told at the moment
they submit — not only in a linked policy. Sits directly above the button or
directly under it, above the existing reassurance line.

**Notice text:**

> *By sending your details you're asking us to pass them to a limestone
> contractor so they can contact you about your job. We use them only for that.
> See our [privacy policy](/privacy).*

**Recommended: an explicit consent checkbox** (unticked by default), because the
details are passed to a third party and the contractor will make contact:

> ☐ *I'm happy for my details to be passed to a limestone contractor to contact
> me about this job.*

> **[NEEDS INPUT]** Decide with the operator whether to use the notice alone or
> the notice plus checkbox. The checkbox is the safer basis for the contractor's
> follow-up contact. Either way, the `/privacy` link must be live before the form
> goes live.

## 4 & 5. About page — role downplayed, voice made consistent

Both fixes land in the same section, so the whole "Who runs this" and "What this
business does" copy is restated here. Voice is now first person plural
throughout; the personal employment history (named employer, job title, exact

Replace the Level 2 About page sections **"Who runs this"** and the first two

---

## H2: Who runs this

Perth Limestone Group is run by [OPERATOR NAME] from [NEEDS INPUT: suburb],
Perth. It is a registered business name, [BN NUMBER], operating under
ABN [ABN].

The person behind it has a background in the concrete and construction materials
industry, which is where the footing, aggregate and concrete detail on this site
comes from. We have not laid limestone block and do not claim to. For how a
specific wall gets built, the contractor is the one to ask.

## H2: What this business does

We take enquiries from Perth homeowners about limestone retaining walls, boundary
walls and fencing, and we connect them with a limestone contractor who can quote
and build the job.

We do not build, excavate, lay block, attend site, quote, inspect or certify
anything. There is no crew and no equipment. Every physical part of a job is done
by the contractor.

The other thing we do is research. The [council approval guide](/retaining-wall-approval-perth)
was put together by contacting each Perth local government individually, because
nobody had published a per council reference and homeowners kept being given a
single number that is not true across the metro area. Every row records when it
was checked.

## 7. FAQ schema — clarification (no copy change)

The gap I flagged is small and needs no change to the copy. Google restricted
FAQ rich results (the expandable Q&A shown directly in search) to authoritative
government and health sites around 2023, so `FAQPage` markup on a site like this
will not earn a rich snippet. It is still valid structured data, it is harmless,
and it helps machines parse the page — so keep it. Just don't count on it for a
search-results feature. No action required beyond knowing that.
